# PropAgent - Enterprise Real Estate Platform
## Comprehensive Feature Specification for South Africa

---

## 1. FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT & TRUST ACCOUNTING

### 1.1 Automated Client Accounting
- **Commission Distribution**: Automated payment to agents, contractors, and recipients based on configurable business rules
- **Same-Day Processing**: Process commission payments on the same day tenant pays rent
- **Landlord Payments**: Automated rental disbursements to property owners
- **Contractor Payments**: Scheduled payments to maintenance contractors and service providers
- **Multi-Party Split Payments**: Support for complex commission splits and referral fees
- **Payout Recording**: Detailed tracking of internal/external commissions, royalties, and referral fees

### 1.2 Trust Account Management
- **FICA Compliance**: Built-in identity verification, credit checks, and sanction screening
- **Secure Fund Handling**: Enterprise-grade security for client money management
- **Audit Trail**: Complete transaction history with digital signatures
- **Account Reconciliation**: Automated bank reconciliation tools
- **Financial Reporting**: Comprehensive trust account statements and reports

### 1.3 Payment Processing
- **Multiple Payment Methods**: Support for EFT, credit card, debit order, and cash
- **Payment Gateway Integration**: Secure online payment processing
- **Arrears Management**: Live payment status tracking with early warning system
- **Proactive Reminders**: Automated email and SMS payment reminders
- **Payment History**: Complete payment tracking per tenant/buyer/seller
