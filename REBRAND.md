## 10. Do's & Don'ts

### Do ✅
- Use Deep Navy as dominant brand color
- Pair Playfair Display with Inter
- Use Gold for premium indicators
- Keep designs clean and professional
- Use Bright Blue for interactive elements

### Don't ❌
- Use more than 20% Gold
- Combine with playful typefaces
- Use gradients on brand elements
- Place Navy text on dark backgrounds
- Mix with colors outside palette
